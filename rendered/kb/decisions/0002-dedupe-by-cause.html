0002 · Duplicates share a cause, not a subject

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Replaces: 0001 once accepted

Context

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. In #4471 that put three problems with the same symptom into one ticket marked known (the split). Subjects describe the symptom; engineering needs one ticket per cause.

Decision

Merge on subject during an incident, as now. Before a ticket is marked known, group it by cause (causes.ts, plus what the desk read in each report) and split off every cause that is not the ticket’s own.

Consequences

  • Marking a big ticket known takes one extra pass, run by the kb, not by hand.
  • A fix closes only the reports it causes; the rest stay visible.

How a report is placed

  1. Count each report once, by id: the export repeats some rows across pages.
  2. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk notes) is shift, whatever its tenant.
  3. Otherwise its cause comes from its tenant, not from intake’s tag (causes.ts): a booking-v3 tenant through the form is hint; any other day-first tenant is parser.

Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. Do not split by it: it disagrees with the cause for many reports in #4471.

What each ticket needs

  • parser: read dates in the tenant’s own order (Intl for their locale), never month first.
  • hint: booking-v3 must print the hint in the tenant’s order, or read what it prints.
  • shift: store and send the booking’s date in the tenant’s time zone; check invites across zones and around clock changes.
